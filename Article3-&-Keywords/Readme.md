## Abstract (Summary)
This paper provides a comprehensive review of the applications of Reinforcement Learning (RL) in real-world industrial systems. The authors analyze the challenges of transferring RL algorithms from simulation environments to practical, real-world scenarios, highlighting the technical barriers such as sample efficiency, scalability, and robustness. The study categorizes and summarizes various algorithmic, statistical, and transfer-based techniques that have been proposed to bridge the "sim-to-real" gap. Furthermore, it reviews RL applications in autonomous control, robotics, the Internet of Things (IoT), and supply chain management. The conclusion emphasizes that although RL has shown significant progress in simulation and isolated tasks, its widespread industrial adoption still requires overcoming challenges related to interpretability and real-world adaptability.
