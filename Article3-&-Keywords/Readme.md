## Abstract
This article provides a detailed review of the applications of Reinforcement Learning (RL) in industrial and automation systems. It examines key challenges in transferring RL algorithms from simulation to real-world environments, addressing limitations in scalability, robustness, and interpretability. The study categorizes techniques such as algorithmic modifications, statistical approaches, and sim-to-real transfer strategies. RL applications in robotics, autonomous control, the Internet of Things (AIoT), and supply chain management are analyzed, highlighting their potential to enhance automation and decision-making in complex industrial processes.
