## Abstract
This review presents a systematic bibliometric and qualitative analysis of deep learning-based object detection algorithms in medical imaging. It explores trends across imaging modalities such as CT, pathology, and endoscopy, and anatomical domains including digital pathology and microscopy. The study analyzes 311 publications using PRISMA methodology, highlighting algorithm diversity, dataset characteristics, and international research contributions. Findings reveal increasing adoption of object detection models in medicine, with notable gaps in prospective studies and dataset availability. The paper identifies key contributors, journals, and countries, offering insights for future research and optimization in clinical applications.
