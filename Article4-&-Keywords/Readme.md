# Application of Explainable AI to Healthcare: A Review

**Authors:** Samuel Gbenga Faluyi, Yousra Chabchoub, Maurras Ulbricht Togbe, Jérémie Sublime  
**Affiliation:** ISEP, Issy Les Moulineaux, France  
**Conference:** IDDM'24: 7th International Conference on Informatics & Data-Driven Medicine, Birmingham, UK, November 14–16, 2024  
**Type:** Review Article  

## Abstract
This review explores the integration of machine learning (ML) and deep learning (DL) techniques in healthcare, emphasizing the importance of explainability in clinical applications. It analyzes models for prediction, classification, regression, and image processing, and evaluates explainability frameworks such as Alibi, InterpretML, and ExplainerDashboard. Case studies include disease prediction, diabetes onset, and heart condition monitoring. The study highlights the role of electronic health records (EHR) and IoT-based data collection, recommending ensemble learning strategies and interpretable AI models for future research.
